About the stay

Built to sit quietly inside the landscape.

Jaldapara Ecostay is a small eco-retreat in Madarihat, in the Dooars belt of North Bengal — close enough to Jaldapara National Park that guided safaris, birdwatching walks, and village tours all start right from the property.

Every cottage is built with sustainable materials and gives you uninterrupted electricity, free high-speed Wi-Fi, and heaters for cool Dooars evenings — comfort without losing the point of being here: the forest, right outside.
